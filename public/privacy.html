Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

The short version: ResumeTailor has no accounts, no database, and no tracking cookies. Your resume is sent to an AI service to be rewritten, the result comes straight back to your browser, and nothing about it is saved on our servers. Anything you save stays on your own device.

What ResumeTailor does with your resume

When you upload a PDF and click Analyze, the file is sent to our server, where the text is extracted. If the PDF is a scan with no selectable text, the pages are rendered as images and read using OCR software running on that same server — the images are never sent anywhere else, and the temporary files holding them are deleted the moment the request finishes.

The extracted text and the job description you pasted are then sent to Groq, which runs the AI model that rewrites your resume (llama-3.3-70b-versatile). The tailored result is returned to your browser. At no point is any of it written to a database, because ResumeTailor does not have one.

What gets stored, and where

Everything ResumeTailor saves is stored in your own browser using localStorage. Nothing is stored on our servers.

WhatWhereHow long
Your working session Your browser (rt:session:v1) Until you click Reset or clear your browser data
Saved versions Your browser (rt:versions:v1) Until you delete them or clear your browser data
Uploaded PDF Held in memory during the request only Discarded when the request completes

Because this data lives on your device, anyone with access to your browser profile can read it. On a shared or public computer, use the Reset button when you are finished.

Services we rely on

ResumeTailor is a small project built on a few third-party services. Each one handles data under its own privacy policy:

  • Groq — runs the AI model. Receives your resume text and the job description. We do not control how long Groq retains request data; see the Groq privacy policy.
  • Render — hosts the server that extracts text from your PDF. Standard server logs record request metadata such as IP address and timestamp. Resume contents are not written to those logs.
  • Cloudflare — serves the site and provides analytics. Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookieless and reports only aggregate page views, not individuals.
  • Google Fonts — delivers the site's typeface. Google receives your IP address as part of that request.
  • Buy Me a Coffee — only if you click the support link. Payment is handled entirely on their site; ResumeTailor never sees your card details, billing address, or name.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ups, or passwords
  • No advertising, ad networks, or tracking cookies
  • No selling, renting, or sharing your data with data brokers
  • No emailing you — we never collect your address for contact
  • No human at ResumeTailor reads your resume

The email and phone number on your resume are processed only as text to be placed onto your tailored resume. They are never extracted for any other purpose.

Deleting your data

There is nothing on our servers to delete, so you are in full control. Clicking Reset in the app clears your current session, and deleting a saved version removes it immediately. Clearing your browser's site data for this domain removes everything ResumeTailor has stored.

If you are in the EU, UK, or California and want to exercise a data right, you are welcome to contact us — though in most cases the answer will be that we hold nothing about you to begin with.

Children

ResumeTailor is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly process their information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your information is handled — for example, if ResumeTailor ever starts storing resumes on the server — the date at the top will be updated and the change described here.
